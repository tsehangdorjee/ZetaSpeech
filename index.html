01 / OVERVIEW

Built for Tibetan speech synthesis

ZetaSpeech brings together real-world speech from three major Tibetan dialects. Audio screening, corrected transcriptions and speaker identity information support research in high-quality Tibetan speech synthesis.

01

Three Tibetan dialects

Amdo, Ü-Tsang and Kham, preserving the distinctive speech characteristics of each dialect.

02

Quality for synthesis

Attention to recording quality, text–speech correspondence and segmentation that respects meaning.

03

Consistent speaker identities

Unified speaker labels connect recordings across files and support voice modeling.
